# AHAM: consciousness, body, mind and artificial ownership

## Official theme

> Consciousness is beyond the body and mind. Show artificial sense of Self in body and mind.

The supplied participant guidance asks teams to explore agency, identity, body ownership or the appearance of a self, allows simplified electronics/software, and requires the connection to be visible to a spectator.

Our approach is **a human participant interacting with an artificial body representation**. "Artificial sense of Self" here means that the representation and its cues may influence the person's agency or ownership. Neither the software nor ESP has demonstrated subjective experience.

## Three layers to show together

| Layer | In AHAM | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| **Body / representation** | Real movement becomes a virtual hand. | Actual finger/wrist motion and interaction. |
| **Mind / identification** | The participant may experience "I control it" or "this is mine." | Their own spoken report; it cannot be inferred from tracking. |
| **Awareness / consciousness** | The participant notices the hand and their thought/feeling about it. | Guided reflection on the Tattva; no consciousness sensor or score. |

The philosophical reading distinguishes known body/mind contents from knowing awareness. The prototype gives a concrete experience for reflection; it does not prove the metaphysical claim.

## The key bridge

**Movement correspondence -> possible agency -> possible ownership -> identification -> reflection on awareness of that identification.**

Agency concerns causing an action. Ownership concerns experiencing a body part as one's own. Identity is broader; a hand illusion does not show a person's whole identity changed. Consciousness concerns subjective awareness. Tracking and ESP receipt are engineering facts, not ownership or consciousness measurements.

A participant can notice a moving hand and the thought "that feels like mine." Ask what is aware of both. This is a reflective question, not a test that forces a philosophical answer. Preserve reports of no ownership.

The original virtual-strike analogy connects identification to an event becoming personally significant. The current setup has no physical motor feedback, and a dedicated strike event is unverified. Demonstrate movement/contact and actual request receipt honestly. A future verified motor adds a bodily cue, not evidence of injury or realistic touch.

## Main shloka: field and knower

**Bhagavad Gita 13.2** in the cited edition:

> इदं शरीरं कौन्तेय क्षेत्रमित्यभिधीयते।
> एतद्यो वेत्ति तं प्राहुः क्षेत्रज्ञ इति तद्विदः॥

Roman transliteration:

> idaṃ śarīraṃ kaunteya kṣetram ity abhidhīyate |
> etad yo vetti taṃ prāhuḥ kṣetrajña iti tad-vidaḥ ||

Plain meaning, paraphrased: the body is called the field; the one who knows it is called the knower of the field. [Verse and commentary](https://www.holy-bhagavad-gita.org/chapter/13/verse/2/), [IIT Kanpur Gita Supersite](https://www.gitasupersite.iitk.ac.in/srimad?choose=1&ecsiva=1&etassa=1&field_chapter_value=13&field_nsutra_value=2&language=dv&setgb=1).

Connection: **"The virtual hand is observed. The thought 'my hand' is also noticed. The theme asks us to distinguish what is known from the awareness knowing it."** This is an illustration: a rendering is not literally the full kṣetra, and the microcontroller is not kṣetrajña. Use 13.2 consistently with the cited edition; numbering can differ between editions.

## Optional supporting shloka: passing sensory experience

**Bhagavad Gita 2.14:**

> मात्रास्पर्शास्तु कौन्तेय शीतोष्णसुखदुःखदाः।
> आगमापायिनोऽनित्यास्तांस्तितिक्षस्व भारत॥

Roman transliteration:

> mātrā-sparśās tu kaunteya śītoṣṇa-sukha-duḥkha-dāḥ |
> āgamāpāyino 'nityās tāṃs titikṣasva bhārata ||

Plain meaning, paraphrased: sensory contacts give rise to heat/cold and pleasure/pain; these arise and pass and should be borne with steadiness. [Verse and commentary](https://www.holy-bhagavad-gita.org/chapter/2/verse/14/).

Connection: **"A contact event and a thought about it can begin and end. We can notice their changes rather than equating each passing state with the entire Self."** This supports the original pleasure/pain analogy; 13.2 is the primary verse for body and knower. Our requests-only demo does not induce pain or experimentally verify this teaching.

Use one verse well rather than several without explanation. Sanskrit is optional: meaning and a visible connection matter more than recitation.

## What can be established now

- The model changes with actual movement.
- Virtual contact/holding changes computed requests, which ESP receives.
- The participant can report agency, ownership, neither or another experience.
- The team can explain representation -> identification -> awareness visibly.

The demo cannot establish consciousness outside the physical body, consciousness in electronics, realistic pain, calibrated force or a change in the participant's entire identity.

Published virtual-hand research supports movement synchrony as a rationale for studying ownership, not a guarantee that AHAM produces it. [Original study](https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/id/eprint/80802/1/80802.pdf). Our trial sheet is informal; use actual reports without causal or population claims.

Keep the statements distinct: **the build explores a human sense of agency/ownership; the Tattva offers reflection on consciousness beyond body and mind.**
